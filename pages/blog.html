Patrice Waverly-Fontaine
Palm Beach, FL
Jun 7

Darlings, you simply won't believe the week I've had—Tuesday's match at The Bath & Tennis Club was absolute perfection until Bunny showed up twenty minutes late (something about her facial at The Breakers running over, though between us, I suspect she was really having those little injections we don't discuss). I was positively fuming but managed to stay composed in my new Coastal Serve Tank from Pickleball Florida USA, which I paired with my Loro Piana linen skirt—the combination was so chic that even Mitsy Carmichael asked where I got it, and you know she never compliments anyone. Carlos has me on this new agility drill that's absolutely murder on the quads, but it paid off when we completely demolished the Patterson sisters 11-3, 11-4, and I executed this backhand slice with my new Selkirk paddle that literally made everyone at the net gasp.

Geoffrey took me to Ta-boo for lunch afterward where I ran into Margaux (unannounced, naturally, darling), who informed me she's moving *back* to Palm Beach after her divorce is finalized—more on that crisis next week, I simply cannot even begin to process. I've been hydrating religiously with my Coastal Hydration Bottle paired with my Hermès charm because Carlos insists proper hydration is the secret to my improved stamina on the court, and honestly, I do feel rather invincible lately. Friday's round-robin at Phipps Ocean Park was slightly less glamorous but terribly fun, and I may have discovered the most divine pickleball strategy that I'm dying to share—but you'll simply have to wait until next week because Bunny made me swear not to reveal our secret weapon until after the club tournament.

Nicolette Hargrove
Naples, FL
Jun 6

Honestly, this week at Naples Grande has been absolutely *divine*—Thomas had me working on my third shot drop until my arm nearly fell off, but I cannot tell you how satisfying it was when I finally nailed three in a row during yesterday's doubles match! Camille flew in from Atlanta for a long weekend, and between games we discovered the most incredible grilled branzino at Mediterrano that I'm still dreaming about. I've been living in my new Pickleball Florida USA performance skort paired with an Alo crop tank, and the way the fabric moves during those quick lateral shifts at the net is just *chef's kiss*. Derek even noticed, though let's be real, he was probably just happy I wasn't critiquing his serve for five whole minutes. Sweet man tries so hard, bless him.

Speaking of gear that's made a difference, I finally caved and ordered those FROMM Pro Overgrips from Amazon after seeing literally everyone at Pelican Bay using them, and honestly? Game changer for these Florida humidity levels. After our Saturday morning drill session, Camille and I took Rosie for the longest walk through Venetian Village—she found every single water bowl at every shop, naturally—and then treated ourselves to the most gorgeous grain bowls at Seed to Table because we'd absolutely earned it. I'm testing out a new paddle next week that Thomas is *convinced* will improve my backhand consistency, and I cannot wait to report back on whether he's right or if this is just another one of his schemes to get me to stop arguing about technique!

Vivian Kowalski-Reed
The Villages, FL
Jun 5

Honey, let me tell you about the week I had—started Monday morning at Knudson Courts where Kenny showed up in a brand new shirt from Pickleball Florida USA, one of those moisture-wicking jobs with the palm tree design, and I swear it made him think he'd gained half a point on his rating. He's still a 3.0 on his best day, but that shirt did keep him from sweating through his delusions, so I'll give him that. Frank had already finished his 7am session—the man treats pickleball like it's a union job with a punch clock—so Dottie and I took on these snowbirds from Michigan at Richmond Courts who kept calling the kitchen "the no-volley zone" like we just got here yesterday. We smoked them 11-3, 11-5, then headed to Katie Belles for celebratory ice cream because at 68, I've earned the right to have dessert at 9:30 in the morning. Marge saw us from her golf cart in the parking lot and made that face she makes, you know the one, like she just bit into a lemon that owed her money.

Listen, by Thursday my paddle grip was so worn out I almost lost it during a slam at Tierra Del Sol, so I hopped on my cart—decked out in Cleveland Browns decals because some things you don't leave behind—and zipped over to the Publix on Rolling Acres to grab some essentials, then got home and ordered one of those Gamma foam replacement grips from Amazon. Two-day shipping is a miracle for us old folks who remember when you had to wait six weeks for something from the Sears catalog. Friday's round-robin was peak Villages chaos: someone's golf cart alarm kept going off, a gecko ran across Court 3 causing a let call that sparked a ten-minute debate, and Frank told the same story about rewiring a house in Parma in 1983 that he's told forty-seven times. Dottie and I are playing in the tournament at Lake Sumter Landing next week, and if Kenny signs up thinking that new shirt makes him competitive, you better believe I'll have stories worth reading.

Stella Marchetti
Sarasota, FL
Jun 4

Here's the thing: I spent four mornings this week at Pompano Park getting absolutely demolished by the Tuesday crew, and I'm not going to lie, my ego took more hits than my backhand. Diane kept trying to be encouraging with her "good try, Stella" after every errant shot, which somehow made it worse, while Gabi was cackling from the sidelines like I was performing stand-up comedy. Marco showed up Wednesday looking like he'd rather be reviewing blueprints, proceeded to nail every third shot drop like he invented the damn thing, then complained about his knee for twenty minutes. I finally dragged everyone to Piccolo Italian Market afterward because if I'm going to suffer, we're at least eating proper mortadella. On the gear front, I finally replaced my ratty paddle cover with one of those Pickleball Florida USA neoprene sleeves—the design is clean, no tacky flamingos or aggressive fonts, just straightforward protection that doesn't scream "I'm a middle-aged person obsessed with a sport that sounds like a condiment."

The real controversy this week? I told the crew they all need to get actual court shoes instead of wearing whatever expired New Balance they found in their garage, specifically something like the ASICS Gel-Rocket 11s from Amazon, because watching people slip around like newborn deer is stressing me out. You'd think I suggested they all get matching tattoos. But I'm putting my foot down—literally, with proper lateral support—because someone's going to blow out an ankle doing some hero dive at the kitchen line, and it's probably going to be me. Friday at Bay Front was gorgeous though, that bay breeze almost made me forget I'm basically paying in sweat and frustration to hit a plastic ball over a net. Next week I'm either breaking through this plateau or switching to bocce, and honestly I haven't decided which would disappoint Gabi more.
