Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

PickleballFloridaUSA.com ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Email address. If you sign up for our newsletter or download a free guide, we collect the email address you provide. That's the only personal information we ask for.

Analytics data. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on site, approximate location (city level), and device type. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you our email newsletter and the resources you request
  • To understand which content is most useful so we can create more of it
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Email Communications

Our emails are sent through Brevo, our email service provider. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time with one click. We comply with the CAN-SPAM Act.

Cookies

We use cookies (small text files stored by your browser) for analytics purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work normally.

Affiliate Disclosure

PickleballFloridaUSA.com is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. When you click a product link on our site and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are genuinely useful to Florida pickleball players.

Our Shop

Products in our shop are sold through our Etsy storefront. Purchases made on Etsy are governed by Etsy's privacy policy; we never see or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy: Google Analytics (site analytics), Google Maps (court location maps), Brevo (email delivery), Amazon Associates (affiliate links), and Etsy (shop). Links to external websites are provided for your convenience; we are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices & Data Requests

You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time. If you would like your email address removed from our records entirely, or have any question about this policy, contact us and we'll take care of it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
