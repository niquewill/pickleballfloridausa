Florida Pickleball Apparel — FAQ

Where is the Coastal Collection made and shipped from?
The Coastal Collection is available through our Etsy storefront. All orders are processed and shipped through Etsy's trusted fulfillment network. Shipping times and options are shown at checkout on Etsy.
What makes this pickleball apparel designed for Florida?
Every piece in the Coastal Collection is designed with Florida's court conditions in mind — lightweight breathable fabrics for the heat and humidity, coastal color palettes inspired by Florida's turquoise waters and coral sunsets, and a resort-lifestyle aesthetic that feels at home from the pickleball court to the club.
Do you offer Florida pickleball gift ideas?
Absolutely — the Coastal Collection makes excellent gifts for Florida pickleball players. The Coastal Court Tote, Palm Paddle Tees, and Coastal Hydration Bottles are all popular gift choices. Perfect for birthdays, Father's Day, retirement gifts, and holiday shopping for the pickleball player in your life.
Can I wear these on the pickleball court?
Yes — all apparel in the collection is designed to be court-ready. The tanks and tees are lightweight and breathable, built for active play in Florida's warm climate. The Coastal Court Tote fits paddles, balls, and all your court essentials.
