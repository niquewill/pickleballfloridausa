Pickleball Travel

Pickleball Vacations

Florida resort getaways, guided European pickleball trips and pro events abroad — where to travel with your paddle.

Pickleball travel has grown up. Florida resorts now build whole weekends around clinics and round robins, and a handful of tour companies run guided trips that pair daily court time with sightseeing in Europe. If you have ever wanted to dink your way through Tuscany or the Algarve, this guide shows who is running trips, where they go and what to expect.

Staying closer to home? Start with our guide to Florida pickleball resorts and camps.

Pickleball Vacations — Frequently Asked Questions

Are there pickleball vacations in Europe?
Yes. Companies including Pickleball Trips, Mickle Pickleball Trips and Active Away run guided pickleball holidays in Italy, Spain, Portugal, Croatia, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Greece and Turkey. Most include daily court sessions with coaching or local players, plus sightseeing.
How much does a European pickleball trip cost?
Guided multi-country tours from US operators list roughly $3,950 to $5,950 per person before airfare. Resort-based coached holidays booked through European operators can start under £1,000 per person for a week, depending on the season.
Is there professional pickleball in Europe?
Yes. The PPA Tour launched PPA Tour Europe in August 2026, with a 2026–27 season of 20 events in nine countries, including Spain, Portugal, Italy and the United Kingdom, and the PPA Finals Europe in May 2027.
What is the best Florida pickleball vacation?
For a Florida trip, look at resorts with dedicated courts and programs, such as PGA National in Palm Beach Gardens, Omni Amelia Island and Saddlebrook in Wesley Chapel. Time it with the US Open in Naples each April if you want to watch the pros.
Do I need to be an advanced player for a pickleball trip?
No. Most pickleball trips and resort programs group players by skill level and welcome beginners through advanced players. Check each trip's skill requirements before booking.
