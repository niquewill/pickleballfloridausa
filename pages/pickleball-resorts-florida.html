Florida Pickleball Vacations

Pickleball Resorts in Florida

Hotels with pickleball courts, resort clinics and multi-day camps — everything you need to plan a Florida pickleball vacation.

Florida is the easiest place in the country to build a vacation around pickleball. The big resorts have added dedicated courts, pros and weekly clinics, and the state's best training centers run adult camps through the winter season. Whether you want a beachfront hotel with a few courts or a full week of coaching, this guide covers the Florida pickleball resorts and camps worth booking.

Planning a trip around a big event? Check the Florida pickleball tournament calendar first. The US Open in Naples fills hotels across Collier County every April.

Florida Pickleball Resorts — Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best pickleball resorts in Florida?
Top Florida pickleball resorts include PGA National Resort in Palm Beach Gardens (12 courts, 6 lit), The Boca Raton (12 courts with a certified pro), Omni Amelia Island Resort (7 courts and weekly clinics), Saddlebrook Resort in Wesley Chapel (daily clinics and coached round robins) and Innisbrook Resort near Clearwater.
Which Florida hotels have pickleball courts?
Hotels and resorts with pickleball courts in Florida include PGA National Resort, The Boca Raton, The Breakers in Palm Beach, Omni Amelia Island, Innisbrook, Saddlebrook and Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate. Many smaller beach hotels lack courts, so pairing a hotel with nearby public courts from our Florida court finder is often the best value.
Are there pickleball vacation packages in Florida?
Yes. Several Florida resorts bundle court time and instruction into the stay. Saddlebrook's pickleball program includes daily clinics and court time, and Innisbrook offers group "Pickleball Party Pack" packages. Ask each resort about current packages, since offers change by season.
What are the best pickleball camps in Florida?
Adult camps run by US Sports Camps (Nike) at Saddlebrook Resort and at the USOP National Pickleball Center in Naples are two of the best-known options. For shorter sessions, resort bootcamps at Omni Amelia Island and clinics at PGA National add coaching to a vacation.
When is the best time for a Florida pickleball vacation?
October through April is peak season, with comfortable temperatures and the busiest clinic and tournament calendars. Summer rates are lower, but plan to play early in the morning or at resorts with lit courts for evening play.
