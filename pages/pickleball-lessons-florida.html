Pickleball Lessons & Clinics in Florida

Beginner clinics, private coaching and certified instructors — where to take pickleball lessons across Florida.

Florida Pickleball Lessons — Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I take pickleball lessons in Florida?
Options include free city clinics (Tampa runs free beginner lessons on Mondays), club programs like Ace Pickleball Club's Starter Series, academies such as the USOP National Pickleball Center in Naples, and resort clinics at Omni Amelia Island and PGA National.
Are there free pickleball clinics for beginners in Florida?
Yes. The City of Tampa offers free beginner lessons, The Fort in Fort Lauderdale runs free beginner classes, and Ace Pickleball Club's Starter Series includes a free first session. Many city parks departments run similar programs, so check your local parks and recreation site.
How do I find a certified pickleball instructor in Florida?
Search the coach directories of the certification bodies recognized by USA Pickleball: PPR (Professional Pickleball Registry), PCI (Pickleball Coaching International) and RSPA. IPTPA also has a directory you can filter by state.
How much do pickleball lessons cost in Florida?
Group clinics range from free city programs to roughly $30–40 for a two-hour resort bootcamp. Private lessons with an experienced coach typically cost more, with some Orlando-area academies charging around $200 per hour. Ask about packages, which usually lower the per-lesson price.
How many lessons do I need to learn pickleball?
Most beginners can play a friendly game after one or two clinics. A short series of four to eight sessions, like a club starter program, builds the dinking, third-shot and kitchen-line habits that help you move up a skill level.
