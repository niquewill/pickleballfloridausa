Florida Pickleball Tournaments — Frequently Asked Questions

When are the major pickleball tournaments in Florida in 2026?
Major Florida pickleball events in the 2026–27 season include the PPA Tour's Daytona Beach Open (November 16–22, 2026) and Cape Coral Open (February 1–7, 2027), the APP Tour Championships at The Fort in Fort Lauderdale (December 3–6, 2026), and the US Open Pickleball Championships in Naples (April 17–24, 2027). Florida's peak tournament season runs October through April when snowbird season brings thousands of competitive players to the state.
How do I register for amateur pickleball tournaments in Florida?
Most Florida pickleball tournaments are registered through USA Pickleball's tournament software at usapickleball.org, Pickleballtournaments.com, or directly through the event organizer's website. You'll need a USA Pickleball membership and a DUPR rating for sanctioned events. Registration typically opens 4-8 weeks before the event date.
What skill level do I need to enter a Florida pickleball tournament?
Florida pickleball tournaments offer divisions for all skill levels — from 2.5 beginner through 5.0+ professional. Most recreational tournaments use self-rating or DUPR ratings to place players in appropriate divisions. Many Florida tournaments offer senior divisions (50+, 60+, 70+) as well as mixed doubles, men's doubles, and women's doubles events.
What is the US Open Pickleball Championships in Naples Florida?
The US Open Pickleball Championships held in Naples, Florida is one of the largest and most prestigious pickleball tournaments in the world. It draws thousands of players and spectators from across the globe, features pro divisions with top-ranked players, and includes amateur divisions for recreational players of all skill levels. It is held each April (April 17–24 in 2027) at the Naples Pickleball Center in East Naples Community Park.
Are there beginner pickleball tournaments in Florida?
Yes — many Florida pickleball tournaments welcome beginners with dedicated 2.5 and 3.0 skill level divisions. Community parks and recreation centers across Florida also host informal round robins and fun tournaments that are perfect for newer players. Check the Florida Events tab above for beginner-friendly local events.
What is the best time of year for pickleball tournaments in Florida?
Florida's peak pickleball tournament season runs from October through April — cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and the arrival of snowbirds from northern states make this the prime competitive window. Summer tournaments do exist but outdoor events start early morning to beat the Florida heat. Indoor facilities host year-round tournaments regardless of season.
When is the US Open Pickleball Championships in Naples?
The US Open Pickleball Championships is held every April at East Naples Community Park. The 2027 edition runs April 17–24, after a 10th-anniversary 2026 event, with around 60 courts and amateur and pro divisions. Spectator and registration details are posted at usopenpickleball.com.
Is there an APP Tour event in Florida?
Yes. The APP Tour held the APP Fort Lauderdale Open at The Fort on March 25–29, 2026, and returns for the APP Tour Championships (December 3–6, 2026) and APP Next Fort Lauderdale (December 11–13, 2026). The PPA Tour also plays in Florida, with the Proton Daytona Beach Open at Pictona on November 16–22, 2026 and the Cape Coral Open on February 1–7, 2027. Check theapp.global and ppatour.com for tickets and registration.
