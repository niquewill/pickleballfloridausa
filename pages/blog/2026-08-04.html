The Dink Diaries

Week of August 4, 2026

Four women. Four Florida pickleball towns. One very eventful week.

Patrice Waverly-Fontaine
Palm Beach, FL · Aug 4

Love-Love to Love-All: My Most Scandalous Week on the Courts (And I Mean That in the Best Possible Way)

Darlings, where do I even begin. This week was, as Geoffrey would say while barely looking up from his Financial Times, *a lot.* It started Tuesday at Phipps Ocean Park before the humidity became absolutely criminal, Bunny and I arriving practically at dawn in matching Coastal Serve Tanks from Pickleball Florida USA — mine in the most divine coral that I paired with my Hermès enamel bangle because why on earth would one ever not — and the courts were already buzzing with what I can only describe as the most delicious drama. Apparently someone on the Tuesday morning rotation had been poaching her partner's shots for three weeks running and the whole situation had finally, *finally* come to a head. I said nothing. I saw everything.

Carlos had me doing lateral band walks and wrist stability work before we even touched a paddle, which I find both admirable and slightly punishing, but the man gets results and my dinking game this week was, frankly, immaculate. I have been playing with my JOOLA Ben Johns Hyperion paddle — yes I ordered it from Amazon after Margaux sent me the link approximately eleven times — and I will not pretend I was not skeptical, but darlings, the touch on that paddle is something else entirely. My third-shot drops have become what I can only call *architectural.* Bunny accused me of practicing in secret. I neither confirmed nor denied.

Nicolette Hargrove
Naples, FL · Aug 3

Dinks, Dinners, and Derek's Questionable Backhand: A Week Well Played

Honestly, there are weeks in Naples that just feel like the universe decided to be extra generous, and this past week was absolutely one of them. The mornings have been that perfect soft-gold kind, where the air still holds a little coolness before the sun remembers it lives in Southwest Florida, and I cannot think of a better way to greet that kind of morning than lacing up and heading to the courts. Rosie disagreed, naturally, since she had strong opinions about a second walk before I even had my coffee, but we negotiated and she won, because she always wins.

Tuesday started at Pelican Bay and I cannot overstate how much I love those courts when the light is still low and the ibises are doing their dramatic slow-walk along the edges like tiny disapproving nobles. Thomas had me working on my third-shot drop, which sounds elegant and civilized until you are actually doing it and Thomas is standing there watching with that perfectly patient expression that somehow makes you try harder than any yelling ever could. He adjusted my grip slightly and honestly the difference was immediate and a little humbling, the way good coaching always is. I wore my Coastal Serve Tank from Pickleball Florida USA paired with my Alo high-waist leggings and I cannot tell you how much the right outfit affects my mindset on the court. It sounds silly but it is absolutely true.

Vivian Kowalski-Reed
The Villages, FL · Aug 2

Kenny Tried to Poach My Shot and I Let God Sort It Out (Just Kidding, I Sorted It Out Myself)

Listen, it has been a week and a half out here in The Villages, and I mean that in the best and worst possible ways. Monday started at Knudson Courts where Frank was already out there at 7am sharp, naturally, because that man sets his internal clock to pickleball like other people set it to coffee. I rolled up in the golf cart at 7:22 with my Coastal Hydration Bottle from Pickleball Florida USA clipped to my bag and honey, that thing kept my water cold through three full rounds in 84-degree humidity, which is basically a miracle of engineering. Frank gave me the look. I gave him the look back. We have been married 41 years so neither look means anything anymore.

Tuesday I played with Dottie over at Richmond Courts and let me tell you, Dottie from New Jersey showed up ready for blood. She had her game face on before she even got out of the golf cart. We were up against two gentlemen who clearly thought we were decorative, and honey, we dismantled them so thoroughly that one of them asked if we had ever played tennis. We have. That is actually the problem. Dottie and I celebrated afterward with a late lunch at Katie Belles and I had the grouper sandwich which has absolutely nothing to do with pickleball and everything to do with living your best retired life in Central Florida.

Stella Marchetti
Sarasota, FL · Aug 1

I Came For The Dinks, I Stayed For The Drama (And The Cannoli)

Tuesday at Pompano Park started the way it always does — Gabi pulling into the parking lot blasting something that sounds like a Brazilian carnival crossed with a car alarm, yelling out her window that she called court two before she even had her shoes on. I love this woman. She is the reason I picked up a paddle three years ago and also the reason I sometimes question my life choices at 8am.

Here is the thing about our Tuesday crew — we have got a solid eight to ten regulars who show up no matter what, which in Florida summer heat is either impressive or deeply concerning. Diane was in her zone this week, absolutely surgical from the kitchen line. I am not going to lie, she makes me look like I am playing pickleball for the first time while wearing oven mitts. Retired teachers have this terrifying patience that translates directly into drop shots that die like they have given up on life.
