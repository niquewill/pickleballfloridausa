The Dink Diaries

Week of October 6, 2026

Four women. Four Florida pickleball towns. One very eventful week.

Patrice Waverly-Fontaine
Palm Beach, FL · Oct 6

Love-Love and Lemon Drop Martinis: A Palm Beach Pickleball Week to Remember

Darlings, where do I even begin? This week was absolutely *unhinged* in the most divinely chaotic way, and I have been practically vibrating with things to tell you. It started Monday morning at Phipps Ocean Park, the courts still damp from an overnight shower, the air smelling of salt and possibility. Bunny arrived twelve minutes late wearing what I can only describe as a traffic cone in athleisure form, and I was standing there in my new Coastal Serve Tank from Pickleball Florida USA, paired with the most perfect ivory Lululemon joggers, looking composed while internally *screaming*. We warmed up, Carlos had already texted me my footwork drills for the day, and I thought, yes, this is going to be a week.

And it was, darlings. By Wednesday we had moved our doubles session over to The Bath & Tennis Club, which is always a different energy entirely - more polished, slightly more competitive, the kind of place where someone will absolutely notice if your dink shot is off and will not pretend otherwise. Geoffrey came to watch, bless him, nursing his Cafe Boulud takeaway coffee like the retired hedge fund prince he is, calling out encouragement from behind his sunglasses. I was playing with my JOOLA Ben Johns Hyperion paddle, which I picked up through Amazon after Bunny swore it changed her third-shot drop entirely, and I have to say the control is absolutely extraordinary. My cross-court angles were surgical. Carlos noticed. Bunny noticed. Even the couple on court three noticed.

Nicolette Hargrove
Naples, FL · Oct 5

Dinks, Drinks, and Derek's Disaster at the Net (A Very Full Week in Paradise)

Honestly, I cannot think of a better way to spend a Tuesday morning than watching the sun come up over the courts at Pelican Bay with a cold bottle of water in my hand and Rosie already exhausted from her walk before I even hit my first serve. There is something about this time of year in Naples that just wraps around you like a warm hug, and I am not even slightly sorry for how obsessed I am with every single second of it. The bougainvillea is doing the absolute most right now, the air smells like saltwater and possibility, and my Coastal Hydration Bottle from Pickleball Florida USA has genuinely changed my morning court routine in the best way. I paired it with my favorite Alo leggings this week and felt like a woman who absolutely has her life together, which I cannot always say is entirely accurate.

Thomas had me working on my third shot drop all week, and I cannot tell you how humbling it is to be 54 years old and still have your coach look at you with that patient, loving expression that means you need to do it again, and again, and probably one more time after that. Thomas is endlessly kind about it, which is honestly the only reason I have not retired to the bleachers with a charcuterie board permanently. He broke down my footwork in a way that finally clicked on Wednesday, and by Thursday I was dropping that ball into the kitchen like I was placing a very delicate order at The French on Fifth, which, for the record, I also did this week because life is short and their quiche makes everything better including my backhand.

Vivian Kowalski-Reed
The Villages, FL · Oct 4

Kenny Called An Out Ball In, I Called A Chiropractor, And We Both Survived The Week Barely

Honey, let me tell you this week started with Frank rolling out of the driveway at 6:47 in the golf cart wearing his lucky orange shirt and I did not see him again until the Publix on Rolling Acres run at noon. That man treats 7am pickleball like it is a military operation. Meanwhile I was still looking for my left shoe.

Listen, Tuesday at Knudson Courts was an absolute circus and I mean that with full affection. Kenny showed up with a brand new paddle, a fresh wristband, and the energy of a man who just watched three YouTube videos and now believes he is Ben Johns. He called an out ball in during our third game, and Dottie from New Jersey nearly levitated off the court. Dottie does not accept bad calls quietly. Dottie accepts bad calls the way a thunderstorm accepts a quiet afternoon, which is to say not at all.

Stella Marchetti
Sarasota, FL · Oct 3

I Came for the Dinks, I Stayed for the Drama (And the Arancini)

Tuesday morning at Pompano Park and the crew is already at full volume before I even unlatch my paddle from the Coastal Court Tote. And here is the thing about this bag - I am obsessed with it. The design is clean, the colorway is coastal without being aggressively beach-vacation-screamy, and it actually fits everything without looking like I am hauling moving boxes across the court. Brooklyn Stella approves. That matters.

I am not going to lie, this week humbled me. Diane, my steady-as-a-metronome doubles partner, told me I was poaching too aggressively and she was absolutely right. Diane says things like that the way a retired teacher corrects a run-on sentence - calm, certain, no room for argument. I poached less. We won more. Lesson received.
