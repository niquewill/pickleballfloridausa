# Pickleball Texas USA — Branding Direction

## State Identity
Texas pickleball is big, bold, and community-driven. Austin's outdoor courts, Dallas luxury clubs, Houston's massive player base, and San Antonio's growing scene. The aesthetic is bold, modern, and proudly Texan with a sophisticated edge.

## Color Palette Direction
- **Primary:** Texas Burnt Orange (#BF5700)
- **Secondary:** Lone Star Navy (#1B2A4A)
- **Accent:** Hill Country Sage (#7B9E87)
- **Neutral:** Limestone Cream (#F7F3EC)
- **Dark:** Texas Night (#0F1923)

## Typography Direction
Bold, confident serif headlines. Clean modern sans-serif body. Bigger and bolder than Florida.

## Brand Voice
Bold, community-proud, everything-is-bigger energy, sophisticated but unpretentious, Texas lifestyle focused.

## Key Differentiators from Florida
- Hill Country/urban vs coastal aesthetic
- Burnt orange/limestone vs navy/turquoise color story
- Texas pride and community identity
- Austin/Dallas/Houston urban sophistication
- Bluebonnets and wildflowers vs palm trees

## Target Audience
- Austin outdoor lifestyle players
- Dallas luxury club members
- Houston recreational community players
- San Antonio retirement players
- Texas tournament competitors (massive scene)
- Corporate/country club players
