# Pickleball North Carolina USA — Branding Direction

## State Identity
North Carolina pickleball spans mountain courts in Asheville, coastal courts in the Outer Banks, and the massive Charlotte/Raleigh suburban scene. The aesthetic is naturally beautiful, outdoorsy, and approachable — Blue Ridge Mountains meet Atlantic coastline.

## Color Palette Direction
- **Primary:** Blue Ridge Blue (#3A5F8A)
- **Secondary:** Carolina Forest Green (#3D6B4F)
- **Accent:** Dogwood Pink (#E8A0A0)
- **Neutral:** Mountain Mist (#F0EDE8)
- **Dark:** Smoky Mountain (#1C2B3A)

## Typography Direction
Natural, warm serif headlines. Clean approachable sans-serif. More relaxed than Florida but still premium.

## Brand Voice
Naturally beautiful, outdoorsy, community-warm, mountain-meets-coast lifestyle, approachable premium feel.

## Key Differentiators from Florida
- Mountain/forest vs coastal beach aesthetic
- Blue Ridge blue/forest green vs navy/turquoise color story
- Asheville arts culture vs Florida resort culture
- Four seasons vs year-round summer
- Dogwood and mountain wildflowers vs palm trees
- Both mountain AND coastal court options

## Target Audience
- Asheville outdoor lifestyle players
- Charlotte/Raleigh suburban community players
- Outer Banks coastal vacation players
- Blue Ridge retirement community players
- Mountain resort and retreat players
- Nature-loving recreational players
