# Pickleball Arizona USA — Branding Direction

## State Identity
Arizona pickleball is defined by desert luxury — Scottsdale resort courts, Sedona red rock backdrops, Phoenix year-round sunshine, and a massive snowbird/retiree community. The aesthetic is warm, earthy, and upscale.

## Color Palette Direction
- **Primary:** Terracotta / Adobe Red (#C8553D)
- **Secondary:** Saguaro Green (#4A7C59)
- **Accent:** Desert Gold (#D4A853)
- **Neutral:** Warm Sand (#F5ECD7)
- **Dark:** Desert Night (#2C1810)

## Typography Direction
Same serif/sans-serif combination as Florida but warmer, more Southwest feel.

## Brand Voice
Warm, sun-soaked, resort-luxury, snowbird-friendly, active retirement lifestyle.

## Key Differentiators from Florida
- Desert vs coastal aesthetic
- Terracotta/adobe vs navy/turquoise color story
- Scottsdale/Phoenix resort culture vs beach resort culture
- Sedona red rocks vs palm trees
- Year-round dry heat vs humidity

## Target Audience
- Scottsdale luxury resort players
- Phoenix retirement communities
- Winter snowbirds (November-April)
- Sedona wellness/retreat players
- Competitive tournament players (huge AZ scene)
